01 — about

About me

I'm a B.Sc. student in Electrical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology (2022 – present), originally from Isfahan. My interests sit where theory meets hardware: wireless communications and estimation theory (RIS-aided localization, THz MIMO), RF & microwave design (phased arrays, feed networks, full-wave simulation), biomedical signal processing (ECG and EEG with machine learning) and digital design on FPGAs. My B.Sc. project, supervised by Dr. Rouhollah Amiri, studied localization and synchronization of a mobile user aided by RISs with uncertain positions.

Since 2023 I've been a teaching assistant for six undergraduate courses, and since 2022 I've taught physics olympiad classes. I also served as Educational Officer and Vice Secretary of the Faculty of Electrical Engineering Student Council.

This site also hosts my coursework, including the pre-reports and lab reports for the Software-Defined Radio (SDR) Lab.

Projects

B.Sc. Project · Wireless2026

RIS-Aided Localization & Synchronization under Uncertain RIS Geometry

Joint estimation of a mobile user's position, velocity, frequency error and clock offset with RISs whose positions are only known through a prior: hybrid Cramér–Rao bound via Fisher information and a hybrid MAP/ML estimator with variable projection. Supervised by Dr. Rouhollah Amiri.

  • RIS
  • Hybrid CRLB
  • MAP/ML
  • OFDM
  • MATLAB

Wireless · THz MIMO2026

RMT-Based Channel Estimation for Near-Field THz MIMO

A simulation framework for near-field terahertz MIMO channel estimation using random matrix theory, evaluating BER, SER, spectral efficiency and estimation error across antenna configurations.
